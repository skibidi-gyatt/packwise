# Packwise Cargo
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Cargo planners, dispatch teams and loading supervisors. The one-day hero is a rear-door rigid truck carrying mixed shipments to three stops.

## Product Purpose
Transport more useful cargo with existing capacity while respecting measured dimensions, payload, stacking and operational access. Demonstrate actual baseline-to-optimized differences without fabricated business savings.

## Positioning
Cargo understanding becomes enforceable constraints, a deterministic load plan, and an inspectable 3D digital twin. Astra handles image semantics and language; software calculates placement and physical checks.

## Operating Context
Manifest/manual measurements take precedence over AI estimates. Operators review uncertain image-derived data and issue natural-language changes. The judge-facing flow includes a real first-unload request for P14.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve and evolve the existing Vinext/React/Three.js prototype. One truck mode, generalized cargo and transport-asset contracts. Maximum 30 units. Prototype decision support; no certified axle, dangerous-goods, aircraft, vessel or cargo-securing compliance claims. Runtime Astra remains optional until credentials are configured, as clarified in the preceding request.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing tested collision/support/load code; synthetic manifest in lib/packing/cargo-demo.ts. No customer, revenue, transport-cost or trip-count evidence. All KPIs must be computed.

## Product Principles
- Authoritative measurements win.
- Constraint changes are inspectable before and after solving.
- Feasible cargo count precedes quality scores.
- Show operational effects, not chat transcripts.
- Freeze one coherent demo within the one-day limit.

## Open Decisions
Production identity, tenant access, live enterprise integrations and commercial pricing are outside the hackathon scope. Selecting a rigid truck for the hero and a code-first implementation are engineering decisions under the user's explicit delegation, not claimed user-specified details.
